# Contributions and AI assistance

Umang Agarwal selected the research questions, chose the experimental decision
rules, reviewed the relevant literature, set claim boundaries, and made the
decisions to discard invalid results, stop failed branches, and preregister
replacement experiments.

AI coding assistants were used for implementation scaffolding, execution support,
debugging suggestions, documentation drafts, and adversarial review. Their output
was treated as untrusted until checked against source code and raw artifacts.

Personal verification included:

- reading and correcting intervention and decoding code;
- checking generated responses and headline counts against raw artifacts;
- identifying and correcting a left-padding position-ID defect;
- preserving superseded or failed results rather than relabelling them;
- rerunning parity checks before accepting causal outputs;
- separating post-hoc diagnostics from confirmatory findings.

This disclosure does not imply that every file was typed without assistance. It
states the substantive ownership boundary: research direction, validity
judgments, interpretation, and final claims are the author's responsibility.
